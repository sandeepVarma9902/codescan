Fair billing

Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective September 11, 2026

Single migrations

An unused prepaid migration may be considered for refund on request. Once migration processing begins, infrastructure and processing work has been consumed; failures caused solely by the service should receive either a replacement credit or refund after review.

Subscriptions

Customers can cancel recurring plans through the billing portal. Cancellation stops future renewal and access continues through the paid period unless applicable law requires otherwise. Unused monthly capacity does not roll over.

Duplicate or unauthorized charges

Contact support promptly. Verified duplicate charges are refunded. Unauthorized payments are handled with the payment provider and applicable card-network rules.

How to request help

Provide the account ID, migration ID, charge date, and reason without sending source code or credentials by email. The production support address, response target, and jurisdiction-specific consumer rights must be finalized before public sale.
